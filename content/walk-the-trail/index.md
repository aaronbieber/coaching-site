Walk the Trail is a single month of the same coaching relationship as
Reach the Summit, without the three-month commitment. It costs more than a
single month of Reach the Summit would, because you're paying for the
option to stop after a month, not for less of my attention. Some people
who start here choose to continue into a longer engagement once they've
felt what the work is like. Others get what they need in the month, and
that's the end of it.
